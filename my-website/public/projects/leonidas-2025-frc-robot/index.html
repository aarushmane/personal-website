FIRST Robotics Competition | Leonidas

Leonidas | 2025 FRC Robot

Vision, swerve autonomy, trajectory following, and simulation work for Team 2590’s Leonidas robot, which won 2025 FMA Robbinsville robot performance.

FocusFIRST Robotics Competition | Leonidas
FormatTechnical case study

Leonidas was Team 2590’s 2025 Reefscape robot. The public base project uses Java and AdvantageKit with drive modules, real and simulated hardware IO, PhotonVision, pose estimation, PathPlanner trajectories, and AdvantageScope debugging. Team 2590 won robot performance at the 2025 FMA Robbinsville District Event.

Technical implementation

  • Swerve and hardware abstraction: Worked with Java drive modules and separate real-hardware and simulation IO layers. The same subsystem logic could run against hardware adapters or simulated devices.
  • Odometry and localization: Used gyro and module measurements, odometry threads, pose estimation, and PhotonVision to maintain a field-relative robot state.
  • Autonomous motion: Built and debugged PathPlanner trajectories for multi-step Reefscape scoring and pickup routines.
  • Instrumentation: Used AdvantageKit logs, AdvantageScope layouts, calibration data, tunable numbers, and simulation to diagnose trajectory error and mechanism timing.
  • Vision pipeline: Integrated camera observations into robot-state estimation and separated vision processing from drivetrain control.
  • Technical communication: Presented architecture, debugging evidence, and design decisions to judges and partners.

Awards

  • Robot Performance Winner (2025 FMA Robbinsville District Event): Leonidas earned the event’s robot-performance recognition through consistent autonomous and teleoperated execution.
