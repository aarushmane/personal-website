Kronos | 2024 FRC Robot

Saturday, June 1, 2024

Kronos was Team 2590’s 2024 Crescendo robot. I worked on the Java/WPILib command-based architecture. It separated mechanisms into reusable subsystems and composed match behavior through command factories and autonomous builders. Team 2590 won its division at the 2024 FIRST World Championship.

Technical implementation

  • Command-based coordination: Built and tested Java autonomous routines from PathPlanner paths and reusable command sequences. Used factories to coordinate drive, shooter, conveyor, elevator-arm, intake, and climb actions.
  • Subsystem architecture: Worked across mechanism subsystems and shared constants. This let driver control, autonomous routines, and mechanism sequencing evolve independently.
  • Vision and field awareness: Used PhotonVision and pose-estimation utilities to connect camera measurements to field-aware behavior.
  • Autonomous iteration: Used path files, tunable parameters, characterization commands, simulation, and data logs to refine control values between matches.
  • Competition workflow: Tested full match routines and coordinated software integration with electrical and mechanical work.

Awards

  • FIRST World Championship Division Winner (2024): Team 2590 won its division at the 2024 FIRST World Championship with Kronos.
